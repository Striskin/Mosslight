GAME GOAL
An original medieval 2D top-down exploration action RPG in C++17 and raylib only.
Cozy villages and inns contrast with deliberate, readable fights in woods,
caves, ruined shrines and a knight-held fort. Player freedom, clear telegraphs,
small dependencies and stable gameplay matter more than scale. Keep original
names, stories, UI and pixel artwork. Reference games inspire broad atmosphere,
not copied characters, assets, layouts, music or stories. No large engine or ECS.

CURRENT STORY AND WORLD
Keeper Aven in Hearthmere asks for an Ember Seed from Fernwake Wood and Moon Dew
from Stillwater Cave. Offer both at the Quiet Bell Shrine, then defeat the Hollow
Warden and return its Bellheart to Aven. The optional Wandering Hollow contains
a copper blade. Talla trades four glow fragments for a travelling coat.
NEW: The Ember & Anvil smithy occupies the northeast village house; the Copper
Kettle inn is the northwest house. Greywatch Bailey lies east beyond the forest.
Its oathless knights guard a chest of knight plate. Roadwardens teach combat.

COMPLETED SYSTEMS
- Title, new/continue with overwrite confirmation, pause, death and ending.
- Smooth eight-direction walking/running, facing, tile collisions and camera.
- Nine regions: eight handcrafted maps plus one deterministic six-room cave.
  One full map is loaded at a time; only small persistence records are retained.
- Original pixel sprites/shapes, 480x270 virtual canvas, integer scaling,
  letterboxing, generated sound cues/ambience, dialogue, prompts and journal.
- Quest, persistent chests, checkpoint lanterns, death/respawn and save/load.
- Fists, sword and bow, selected with 1/2/3. Fists are fast/short range, sword
  cuts have windup/active/recovery, bows consume arrows and use swept collision.
- Heavy attacks break knight guards, stagger foes, cost more stamina and commit
  the player longer. Staggered targets take one extra damage per hit.
- Directional guard: the first 0.16 seconds give a timed block and stagger the
  attacker. Normal blocks cost more stamina; exhaustion breaks guard. RMB aims
  bows or turns facing while moving/guarding. Bow users cannot guard.
- Stamina costs for attacks, dodge, guard and sprint, with recovery between
  actions. Attacks cannot be cancelled into guarding or dodging.
- Four regular enemy types: bramblekin, lantern moth, ironback beetle and
  oathless shield knight. Hollow Warden has charges, volleys and two phases.
- Copper blade (2 sword damage), knight longsword (3), moss coat (8 hearts),
  knight plate (10 hearts, reduces heavy hits by 1, movement 9% slower, higher
  dodge stamina cost). Strongest sword upgrades and first-time armor upgrades
  equip automatically. Satchel Enter changes/removes owned armor. Armor swaps
  clamp current health, never heal. Talla offers light coats even to plate owners.
- Runtime enemy focus uses stable spawn ids. F/middle mouse locks/releases, R
  cycles, RMB releases into manual aim. Movement strafes around the target;
  walls, distance, death, rest and region changes release focus. Camera frames
  both combatants; a gold reticle and health bar mark the selected enemy.
- F1/H field guide has controls and combat pages; W/S changes page. Opens from
  title, play and menus, pauses simulation, and restores the previous screen.
  Pause exposes this as Controls / Legend. Each page has a clear controls or
  combat title, and the pause navigation hint fits above short notifications.
- Physical searchable corpses. E collects coins and materials, not automatic
  kill rewards. Beetles yield shells, others fragments. Leftovers stay if full.
- Coins, two real merchant screens, buy/sell transactions with capacity/funds
  checks. Shells sell for 6 coins, fragments for 2. Quest items cannot be sold.
- Two enterable houses with interiors, forges/hearths, shelves, counters, tables,
  rugs and doors. Inn hearth is a real checkpoint. Remaining cottage is scenery.
- Seeded, named visitors wander in village/inn with collision-aware local motion,
  stop near the player and offer six original practical lore conversations.
- Version-3 saves include coins, equipment/selected weapon and armor, stamina, quest,
  chests, cave seed, checkpoints, defeated spawn IDs and pending loot by region.
- Version-1 and version-2 migration preserves previous strongest worn armor.
  Version-1 migration also preserves old progress and gives 24 coins/12 arrows,
  once. New games also start with 24 coins/12 arrows. Existing journeys work.
- Bounded/validated/checksummed saves, atomic replace, valid previous backup,
  recovery, and visible save errors. No external services or runtime networking.

IMPORTANT BEHAVIOR
Defeated foes and unclaimed loot survive travel and save/load. Living enemies
reload with full health. Resting or respawning resets encounters and discards
unclaimed loot, while preserving acquired coins/items/quest/chests. A defeated
boss never respawns. The main quest's Bellheart is granted automatically on
victory; optional coins/materials remain in its searchable corpse.
Dialogue, satchel, journal, shops and pause freeze combat. Focus loss pauses play.
This is approachable, not a punitive death/coin-loss game.

ARCHITECTURE AND FILES
src/types.hpp: plain C++ simulation types and stable enum IDs. Append new item
and region IDs; do not reorder old IDs, because maps/saves use them.
src/game.*: screens, interactions, active region, checkpoints and orchestration.
src/player.*, collision.*, enemies.*, combat.*: movement and tactical combat.
src/targeting.*: stable runtime focus, cycling, occlusion/range and facing input.
src/inventory.*, merchant.*: items, gear stats and validated transactions.
src/dialogue.*, npcs.*: original conversations and seeded wandering visitors.
src/maps.*, procedural.*: map parsing and repeatable cave generation.
src/save.*: v3 serialization, v1/v2 migration and world/equipment validation.
src/main.cpp, render.*, ui.*, audio.*: raylib/input/presentation and smoke harness.
data/maps/*.map: handcrafted ASCII grids with exits, doors, NPCs and enemy spawns.
data/items.txt: stable id, quoted name, quoted description, resale price.
data/enemies.txt: stable id, quoted name, health, speed and detection range.
data/shops.txt: merchant id, item id, bundle quantity, price.
data/rooms.txt: procedural templates. scripts/author-maps.ps1 is optional offline
map authoring, never executed by the game. Modify its coordinates if regenerating.
tests/tests.cpp + tests/expansion.hpp + tests/controls.hpp: C++ regression checks.
No added dependencies: C++ standard library plus raylib 5.5.

CONTROLS
WASD/arrows move; Shift run; J/Z attack; L/X heavy strike; K/C guard;
Space dodge; 1 fists; 2 sword; 3 bow; hold RMB to aim/face a target;
F/middle mouse lock/release; R next target; RMB manual aim releases focus;
E talk, enter, search, open, rest; Q tonic; Tab/I satchel; M journal;
Satchel: W/S selects, Enter wears/removes owned armor; F1/H controls legend;
Escape pause > Controls / Legend > Enter; W/S pages; Escape back to pause;
W/S or Up/Down select shop offers or scroll satchel; Enter buy/sell/confirm;
Escape back/pause; F5 save; F10 mute; F11 borderless fullscreen.

BUILD, RUN AND SAVE
Windows: MSYS2 UCRT64 GCC 14.2.0 plus official pinned raylib 5.5 archive.
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/build.ps1 -Test -Smoke
Play build/Mosslight.exe. Keep data/ beside the executable.
Portable package: build/Mosslight-win64.zip, extract the whole folder.
Build script downloads/checks raylib if absent and records its result below.
CMake/Visual Studio alternative is documented but has not been run here.
Normal save: %LOCALAPPDATA%/Mosslight/journey.sav and .bak backup.
Smoke runs use separate build/captures saves and never alter the player's journey.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
This is a playable expansion, not a full-length RPG. One main quest chain and
boss, nine regions, four regular foes, three weapon styles, two merchants.
No controller/remapping, multiple save slots, authored soundtrack, crafting,
schedules or reputation. Sword upgrade selection remains automatic. Simple
original pixel art remains.
Enemy navigation uses local steering, not pathfinding. Visitors are simple
wanderers, not a simulated town economy. Save checksums detect accidental damage,
not intentional editing. Human balance/feel testing remains necessary.
